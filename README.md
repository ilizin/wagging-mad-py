## WeddingMadPy

🐶 In Madrid, dog areas (*áreas caninas or pipicanes*) are designated, fenced-in public spaces located within urban parks and green areas where dogs are legally allowed to run, play, and socialize completely off-leash. Maintained by the city council, these enclosures offer a safe environment away from traffic and general footpaths, helping pet owners integrate their dogs harmoniously into urban life.

🐶 	Many of these modern spaces are equipped with practical amenities like double-gated entry systems for security, dog waste bag dispensers, water fountains, benches for owners, and specialized agility circuits with ramps, jumps, and tunnels to encourage physical and mental exercise. As part of municipal initiatives, the city continues to expand this network across all districts to ensure dogs and residents can enjoy the outdoors together.
